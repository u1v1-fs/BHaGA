Help & guidance

A practical guide to using U1V1 as a consultant-led business assessment, advisory and growth-management workspace.

1. Recommended consulting sequence

  1. Onboard the client and confirm consent, business identity, industry, location and reporting currency.
  2. Diagnose business health using the core assessment, financial baseline, market, sales, operations and digital information.
  3. Validate source records and distinguish actual figures from estimates and missing information.
  4. Stabilise cash flow, profitability, operational controls and material risks before recommending expansion.
  5. Optimise sales, customers, pricing, processes, capacity and technology.
  6. Scale only when the evidence supports readiness; assign owners, dates and measurable KPIs.
  7. Monitor outcomes, update the client record and preserve historical reviews.

2. Enter once and reuse responsibly

  • Use the same saved client record and client selector throughout the modules.
  • Reuse business identity, industry, location, products/services, customer segments, team information, currency and reporting periods wherever applicable.
  • Where a field offers suggestions, select the existing value rather than creating a near-duplicate spelling.
  • Check the reporting date and period before reusing financial figures. A monthly amount must not be treated as annual, and different periods must not be combined.
  • When a value changes, record the effective date and source. Do not silently rewrite historical reports.
  • Never treat an empty field as zero or infer a value that the client has not supplied.

3. Financial data and projections

  • Keep revenue, cash, profit, costs, receivables, inventory and liabilities separate.
  • Confirm whether costs are direct/COGS, fixed or variable before calculating margins and break-even.
  • Use consistent currency and period lengths for comparisons.
  • Check assumptions behind CAC, customer lifetime value, payback, cash runway and break-even outputs.
  • Review the source and confidence level of every important input. Treat rule-based outputs as preliminary decision support, not audited conclusions.

4. Client records and backups

  • Save or update the client after changing the core assessment.
  • Phase-specific reviews are stored on the client record and should be retained when the core assessment is updated.
  • Use Export client portfolio JSON for the complete client portfolio, including phase-specific records and histories.
  • The regular assessment JSON export is not a substitute for the full portfolio backup.
  • Before importing, export the current portfolio. Import replaces the current portfolio after validation and confirmation.
  • Keep dated backup copies in a secure location and periodically test restoration using a separate browser profile.

5. Workflow, plans and reviews

  • Every recommendation should have a clear reason, owner, due date, priority, status and success measure.
  • Do not mark an initiative complete merely because an action was started; record evidence of the result.
  • Use the engagement log for meetings, decisions, follow-ups and manually recorded fees.
  • Compare performance only across equivalent periods and currencies.
  • Record a new dated review rather than editing prior results when measuring progress over time.

6. Privacy, limitations and responsible use

  • Client information is stored in this browser's local storage. It is not encrypted by this HTML application and may be accessible to someone using the same device/browser profile.
  • A local PIN is a casual-access deterrent, not secure authentication. Do not store passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive personal information.
  • Exported reports and JSON files may contain confidential client information. Share only with client permission and store securely.
  • Calculations and rule-based recommendations depend on the quality and completeness of manually entered data. They are not audit, legal, tax, investment or lending advice.
  • Printing, notifications, online synchronisation and external accounting/CRM integrations depend on browser or connected services and may not be available offline.

7. Before ending a consulting session

  1. Confirm the correct client is selected in every module.
  2. Save the core assessment and each relevant phase review.
  3. Check that action owners and due dates are current.
  4. Export a full client portfolio JSON backup after significant changes.
  5. Verify that the backup file exists and has a plausible file size before relying on it.
